About the Chambers

Omuordu Chambers —
Built on Trust & Scholarship

Founded by Prof. Samuel Chisa Dike, Omuordu Chambers has carved a distinct identity in Rivers State's legal landscape. We combine deep academic knowledge with practical courtroom experience to deliver outcomes that matter.

Our chambers specialises in the complex intersection of Nigeria's hydrocarbon industry and its evolving legal framework — making us the counsel of choice for international oil companies, indigenous operators, and local communities navigating the Niger Delta's legal terrain.

  • Member, Association of Environmental Lawyers of Nigeria (AELN)
  • Registered Notaries Public, Rivers State
  • Experienced in NNPC, DPR & NUPRC regulatory compliance
  • Cross-jurisdictional practice — Nigeria & international arbitration

The Principal

Prof. Samuel Chisa Dike

PhD · Legal Practitioner & Oil & Gas Attorney

Prof. Samuel Chisa Dike is a distinguished legal practitioner with a doctoral degree from the University of Aberdeen, Scotland — one of the world's oldest and most respected institutions of learning. His doctoral research deepened expertise in environmental and energy law, areas of critical relevance to Nigeria's oil-rich Niger Delta region.

A member of the Association of Environmental Lawyers of Nigeria (AELN) and a registered Notary Public, Prof. Dike brings together academic distinction and hands-on legal advocacy. He has advised corporations, government bodies, and individuals across complex petroleum, environmental, and commercial matters.

His chambers, Omuordu Chambers, is located in Port Harcourt — the heart of Nigeria's oil economy — strategically positioned to serve clients in the energy sector and beyond.
