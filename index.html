About the Chambers

Prof. S.C. Dike & Co. —
Built on Trust & Scholarship

Founded by Prof. Samuel Chisa Dike, Prof. S.C. Dike & Co. is a leading boutique legal practice in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. We combine rare academic distinction — including a PhD from the University of Aberdeen, UK — with over 37 years of post-qualification practice experience to deliver outcomes that matter.

Our chambers specialises in Energy Law, Oil & Gas, Environmental Law, Dispute Settlement, Nanotechnology & AI advisory, and Data Protection — making us the counsel of choice for corporations, international investors, and indigenous operators navigating Nigeria's complex legal and regulatory terrain.

Energy Law — Academic & Practice Interest

Prof. Dike is deeply engaged in energy resource development, renewable energy resources, energy security and conservation, energy efficiency practices, energy infrastructure, decommissioning and abandonment, and economics, development and management practices. His interest also covers energy regulatory compliances and management, as well as renewable energy development including coal energy, wind, solar, hydro energy, and nuclear energy.

He is an advocate of clean energy transition and has published extensively on Nigeria's energy security challenges and pathways, drawing lessons from the UK, Norway, and Brazil.

Oil & Gas Law — Academic & Practice Interest

Prof. Dike has hands-on expertise in Oil and Gas contracting, upstream, midstream and downstream regulatory activities, documentation and preparation of memoranda of understanding between international oil companies, and the preparation and documentation of Joint Venture agreements, production sharing contracts, and joint development of straddle petroleum and licensing.

His practice also covers oil and gas contracting, decommissioning, abandonment, indigenous communities, and local content issues — making him uniquely positioned to advise both international operators and host communities in Nigeria's Niger Delta.

Environmental Law — Academic & Practice Interest

Prof. Dike's practice and academic interests include international environmental law, regulatory compliances, environmental impact assessment, oil and gas spillage and remediation, and climate change mitigation, adaptation and resilience legal frameworks — covering national, regional and global regimes.

His work also encompasses carbon reporting and reduction methodologies, oil pollution management, control and remediation. He is a strong advocate of environmental justice and has published widely on the intersection of environmental law, human rights, and sustainable development in sub-Saharan Africa.

Sustainable Development

Prof. Dike has conducted extensive research on sustainable development goals (SDGs) and advises on how Nigeria can attain these goals. He has attended and presented papers nationally and internationally at conferences on the sustainable development agenda.

His work covers the nexus between energy development, economic growth, and environmental sustainability in African states — including decoupling strategies and transition to sustainable energy economies, as presented at the 14th International Conference on Sustainable Energy Technologies, Nottingham, UK.

Maritime Law

Prof. Dike's maritime law interests span maritime law development, transportation, Cabotage, arrest of ships, maritime employment, towage, liens, and general and special maritime claims. He has written and presented on the legal impact of oil and gas resources on maritime infrastructure, drawing lessons from the US for Nigeria.

His practice experience includes advising on the shipping and distribution of crude oil in Nigerian waterways, and handling cases involving arrest of vessels before Nigerian courts.

Litigation & Arbitration

As a lawyer, Prof. Dike has handled cases from lower courts to the Supreme Court of Nigeria, covering energy resources, land acquisition, oil and gas pollution claims, compensation claims, insurance claims, electricity consumption cases, and arrest of vessels.

As a Scottish-trained Arbitrator and Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Scotland), he has served as State Arbitrator in disputes involving the Airport Hotel Workers Union, Airport Hotel, and Rivers State Government. He advises on arbitration, mediation, and conciliation on a consultancy basis across the energy and maritime sectors.
